# Simple English

This skill adds to the ground rules for docs and runbooks that people outside the project read.

- Number the steps and give one instruction in each.
- Put a warning before the step it applies to.
- Keep noun chains under four words.
- Keep articles (a, an, the).
- Write active sentences with a named actor.
- Use one term for one concept across the whole document.
- Treat 20 words per sentence as a soft target, because hard word limits make prose choppy.
