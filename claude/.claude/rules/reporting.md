# Reporting on agent work

After multi-phase or subagent work, finish with a state line that stands alone. The line lists only the open, unverified or user-owned items. With nothing open, end on the answer.

Say what changed in the world: committed, pushed, edited, or none. Keep what you did apart from how you checked it, and mark as verified only what you ran. Give the denominator with every ratio, and recompute figures from the record.

When the user asks you to verify or to report on a test, keep the literal commands and their unedited output in a report file in the project's evidence folder. Redact credentials and keep everything else verbatim. Keep such reports in the project's private evidence folder, and write them to a public repo only when asked.

When asked what subagents are doing, list each in one line. Give honest time estimates.

When you correct or revert your own work, say in chat what was wrong and how you fixed it, in one paragraph.
